# Style Me Smart

Create an app that allows me to track my wardrobe and mix and match piecesZ

Take pictures of shirts/ tops and store as one set. Second set of pants/ jeans/ skirts.

Show me mix and match styles like Ann Taylor/ WHBM. Allow me to like or dislike styles. Learn my style from this. Also allow me keep track of what sets I wore and like optionally. Further understand what styles I like so next time I shop I can see what I actually wear and what i do not. Pieces not worn for over 45 days to be added to a segment called Decide: to be marked as sell/ seasonal/ special. 

Sell means ditch. Seasonal means keep but do not surface in matches unless added back manually. (Show that option.) Specail means only to be worn on occasions. Don’t surface unless asked. 

Every day show 4-5 matches that are not similar to colors worn in past 3 days. Skip items marked seasonal and special. Try to surface unworn items but not if style or piece was disliked. Any item disliked more than 7 different days (with no likes at all) add to a section called Unloved. Every month surface them and ask if can move directly to sell section. Allow bulk yes and manually excluding few items. (Use checkbox). Allow adding what was finally worn
